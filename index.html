Search by item, zone or profession. Most materials can also be bought on the Auction House, so these are the ways to get them without paying player prices.

    Camp blueprints

    Every profession makes three camp objects. The first comes from your trainer or the Camping 101 quest at skill 20. Tiers 2 and 3 need a blueprint, which you can loot or buy from other players.

    BlueprintProfessionSkillDropped byWhat it does

    No confirmed source yet for the skill 300 objects: Alchemy Laboratory, Master Forge, Iron Oven, Arcane Forge, Anarchist's Workbench, Plague Doctor's Laboratory, Fishing Hut, Seed Hybridizer, Sewing Machine, Molten Foundry, Trapper's Workbench and Loom. The same goes for the Expert Campfire at skill 220. Several of these also need a one-off reagent such as Golem Heart or Maker's Spark, so check Wowhead after launch.

    Merchant's Favor

    Merchant's Favor is the currency that buys the new recipes. It is per character. Reputation with the same camp comes separately, and many of the better recipes need both.

    New and Scarce materials

    Scarce materials have no node of their own. They appear as a bonus while you gather. The Legacy perk Bountiful Harvest raises your haul by 20% a rank, up to 100%.

    MaterialComes fromGoes into

    Beta testers reported that Frilled Lichen and Pristine Leather stopped appearing once a character passed about level 25, and that gray mobs or starting zones such as Durotar, Dun Morogh and Zephras Isle may not qualify. Blizzard has not confirmed this and results conflict, so test a small sample before planning a long farm.

    Recipe colors

    • Orange gives a point every time.
    • Yellow usually gives a point.
    • Green sometimes gives a point.
    • Gray gives nothing.

    Good pairings

    • Herbalism with Alchemy for gold and your own consumables.
    • Mining with Blacksmithing for plate wearers and melee.
    • Mining with Engineering for PvP.
    • Skinning with Leatherworking for leather and mail wearers.
    • Tailoring with Enchanting, since you can disenchant what you sew.
    • Two gathering professions if you mostly want gold while leveling.

    What changed from Classic

    • Professions have their own window, on K by default, with search, filters and recipe tracking.
    • A starting-zone quest can teach you Mining, Herbalism or Skinning for free.
    • Junior gathering trainers in starting zones teach up to 75 only.
    • Reagent bags get their own bag slot, and gathering versions exist from the start.
    • The six standard healing potions moved from Alchemy to First Aid.
    • Tailoring, Leatherworking and Blacksmithing recipes often gray out sooner, so Classic routes can stall you.
    • Armor from the new recipes often binds on pickup, so pick the craft that makes your armor type.
    • Gnomes now get fewer device failures, and Tauren can grow a copy of a nearby herb. Neither racial adds skill.
    • Ores stack to 20.

    Good to know

    • Unlearn a primary profession with the red X in the Professions window. You lose all progress.
    • Tracking turns off when you die, so turn it back on.
    • Camp object buffs act like class buffs but do not stack with them.
    • Learn the next rank before you craft while away. Without it, you stop gaining skill at the cap.

    Sites disagree on the totals because they read different builds. Blizzard says over 600 new recipes, and data sites count 884 to 959. One site, MetaBot, also lists a thirteenth skill called Comprehension for reading scrolls, while the other guides list 12.
